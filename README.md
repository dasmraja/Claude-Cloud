# Snakes & Ladders

A single-file browser game of Snakes & Ladders. Open `index.html` in any modern browser — no build step or dependencies.

- 2–4 players, each human or computer-controlled
- Classic 10×10 board with 9 ladders and 10 snakes
- Exact roll needed to land on square 100
- Rolling a 6 gives an extra turn
- Press **Space** (or click the button) to roll
